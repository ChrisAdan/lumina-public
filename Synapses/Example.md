- **Birthday:** YYYY-MM-DD

## How we know each other
A sentence or two on context — how you met, how long you've known them, shared history.

## Things they care about
- Interests, hobbies, passions

## Notes
Anything else worth remembering — recent conversations, life events, preferences, inside jokes.
